About Who we are

KIN — Kids In Need — is a student club that levels up our community. We run donation drives, collect school supplies, and host small fundraisers all year. Simple idea, big payoff: a little support changes everything.

  • Help kids in our community
  • Collect school supplies
  • Host clothing drives
  • Run small fundraisers
